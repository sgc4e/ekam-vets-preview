What ekam vets is

ekam vets is an integrated, holistic veterinary clinic at 134, Sector 1, Vidyadhar Nagar, Jaipur 302039, Rajasthan. Modern veterinary medicine is the base. Homeopathy, herbal medicine, physiotherapy and diet are added on top of it, never instead of it. One team keeps one file for each animal.

The clinic serves pet owners in Vidyadhar Nagar, Shastri Nagar, Jhotwara and the rest of Jaipur. Dogs and cats are most of the work. Rabbits, birds and small animals are seen for routine care. It also treats street animals with no consultation charge. The site is in English and Hindi.

Who runs ekam vets

The clinic runs under a hospital name, not a doctor's name. That is a deliberate choice. The practice and its standard are what the clinic asks owners to trust.

A senior veterinary physician and surgeon leads the clinical side. A second vet handles day consultations. A homeopathic medicine expert joins in person or by video. The team also has a veterinary assistant, a physiotherapist, a lab technician, a trained groomer and a front-desk person.

The founders and team have more than a decade of experience in veterinary medicine, surgery and animal homeopathy, including treating street animals across India. The founding vet and the homeopath have treated street animals free of consultation charge since 2018.

The site does not publish individual names. To ask who will see your animal, call the clinic or write to the clinic by email.
