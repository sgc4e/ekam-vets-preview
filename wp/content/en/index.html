Our mission

ekam means one.

One roof, one file, one team. One animal at a time at the centre of it, treated as whole. We built this clinic because a dog in Jaipur can get a diagnosis, a drug and a discharge, and still cannot get the eight weeks after. That gap is where most animals lose their recovery.

This is a private practice. We charge a fair price and we publish it, and the free consultation for street animals is paid for by the clinic itself.

  • Low stress handling. We work on the floor if that is where your animal is calm.
  • The estimate before the treatment. No test and no procedure without your consent.
  • We never edit reports, in either direction, for any reason.
