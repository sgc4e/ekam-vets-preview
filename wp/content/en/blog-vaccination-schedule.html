Puppy vaccination schedule in India, without the confusion

A clear month by month vaccination and deworming schedule for puppies and adult dogs in India, with what each vaccine covers.

Most new puppy owners get three different schedules from four different people. Here is the one we use, and why each part of it exists.

Deworming comes first

Start at two weeks old, then repeat every two weeks until twelve weeks. After that, monthly until six months, then every three months for life. A puppy full of worms does not respond properly to a vaccine, which is why this comes before anything else.

The core puppy schedule

  • 6 to 8 weeks: first multi-component vaccine, covering distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus and parainfluenza
  • 10 to 12 weeks: second dose of the same, usually with leptospirosis added
  • 14 to 16 weeks: third dose, plus the first anti-rabies
  • 12 months: booster of both, then annually for life

In India rabies vaccination is not optional and not negotiable, for your dog and for the people around it.

Cats are not small dogs

Kittens need their own schedule, usually starting at eight or nine weeks with a feline vaccine covering panleukopenia and the respiratory viruses, a second dose three to four weeks later, and rabies from twelve weeks.

The three weeks nobody explains

A puppy is not protected the day it gets the last shot. Immunity takes about two weeks to build. Until two weeks after the final dose, keep your puppy off public ground, away from unknown dogs and out of pet shops.

Parvovirus survives in soil for months. It is the single most common way a well loved, fully purchased, indoor puppy dies in this city.

What to do if the schedule slipped

It happens. Do not start again from zero and do not panic. Bring the card in and we will work out what is still needed. Usually it is one or two doses, not the whole course.

After the first year

Annual boosters, annual deworming review, monthly tick control through summer, and from age eight a twice yearly blood panel. Our annual package covers consult, blood panel, weight and dental review.
