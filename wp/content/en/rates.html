Our prices, in public.

The most common complaint about vets in this city is the bill nobody explained. So here is the whole list. It is printed on the wall at reception, it is on this page, and it is the same number for everyone who walks in.

These prices are indicative. Your exact cost is confirmed at reception and written into an estimate before any treatment starts. Nothing is charged that you have not seen first.

How billing works here

  • You see the estimate before treatment starts.
  • No test, no admission and no procedure without your yes.
  • Every bill is itemised. Ask about any line and we will explain it.
  • Veterinary services are exempt from GST. Grooming carries 18 percent, already included above.
  • Cash, UPI and card. Receipts on WhatsApp if you want them.

Prices reviewed twice a year. Any change goes on the wall before it goes on a bill.

Street animals pay nothing.

Every rate above applies to owned animals. Animals brought in from the street are treated at no charge. Registered feeders and rescuers get a reduced rate for the animals in their care.
