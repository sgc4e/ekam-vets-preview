Services

Everything in one building.

You should not have to drive across Jaipur for an X-ray, then to a second place for a bath, then to a third for a blood test. All of it is here, and all of it goes into the same file.

01

Clinic and medicine

The core of the practice. Dogs and cats are most of our day. Rabbits, birds and small animals are seen too, and we tell you honestly when a case needs a facility we do not have.

  • General consultation and second opinion
  • Vaccination, deworming and preventive schedules
  • Skin, ear, eye and dental cases
  • Wound care, dressing and abscess management
  • Chronic case management for older animals
  • Day admission with observation and fluids

Surgery

Sterilisation, soft tissue and orthopaedic work in a dedicated theatre. We operate when the case needs an operation. When rest, medicine and physiotherapy will get the same result, we say that instead, and we say it before you have paid for anything.

The clinic has a dedicated operation theatre, a second treatment room that doubles as one, and in-patient beds with a kitchen behind them. An animal that needs to stay can stay.

Every surgical estimate is written and signed off by you first.

Who physiotherapy is for

  • Dogs recovering from a fracture or a cruciate surgery
  • Older animals with hip or spine trouble
  • Paralysis and nerve cases after the acute phase
  • Overweight animals whose joints need help before the weight comes off
  • Any animal on long-term painkillers we would like to reduce

02

Physiotherapy

Therapeutic laser, passive range of motion, controlled exercise and a home routine written for your animal. Sessions run in a course, usually two a week for four to six weeks, and progress is measured, not felt.

Very few clinics in Rajasthan offer this. It is the single biggest difference between an animal that walks again and one that manages.

Physiotherapy is sold alongside a consult, never on its own, because a plan without a diagnosis is guesswork.

03

In-house lab and imaging

Blood counts, biochemistry, electrolytes, urine and skin cytology, run here, read here. Digital X-ray and ultrasound in the same building. Most results are back inside the same visit.

The lab serves our patients only. We do not accept outside samples and we do not sign reports for other clinics. That keeps one person accountable for every number we hand you: us.

We do not edit reports. Not to soften a result, not to justify a treatment, not for anybody. If a machine prints a value, that value goes on your paper.

What we run

  • Complete blood count and biochemistry panels
  • Kidney, liver and thyroid profiles
  • Tick fever and parasite screening
  • Urine and faecal analysis
  • Skin scrape and ear cytology
  • Digital X-ray and ultrasound

No test is run without your yes and without you knowing the price.

Grooming includes

  • Bath, blow dry and brush out
  • Nail trim and paw pad care
  • Ear cleaning and eye wipe
  • Sanitary and full body clips
  • Medicated bath on a vet's instruction
  • Tick and flea treatment

04

Grooming

Done in the clinic, by people who work next to vets. A groomer here is trained to stop and call a doctor when a lump, a hot spot, a bad ear or a painful hip shows up under the water. That has caught more early problems than any awareness poster.

No sedation for a routine groom. If an animal cannot be groomed calmly, we stop and tell you why.

05

Homeopathy

Homeopathic remedies are prescribed here inside the veterinary consult, by a registered veterinary surgeon, as one part of a written plan. They sit alongside the medicine. They do not replace it.

Where we use them: chronic skin that keeps returning, anxiety and travel stress, slow recovery in older animals, and cases where an animal is already carrying a heavy drug load we would like to reduce.

Where we do not: infection, trauma, poisoning, an animal that is dehydrated or in pain right now. Those get medicine, immediately, and we will say so plainly.

What we will tell you

  • What the remedy is and what it costs
  • What we expect to see, and by when
  • What the conventional option is, so you can choose
  • When we will stop it, if nothing changes

No animal is put on a homeopathic plan alone when a conventional treatment is the right answer.

Where herbs earn their place

  • Skin washes for recurring itch and hot spots
  • Digestive support after a long antibiotic course
  • Joint support in older animals, with physiotherapy
  • Wound and coat care between visits
  • Appetite and gut settling in convalescence

06

Herbal and natural medicine

Plant based preparations, used where they help and named where they are used. You get told what is in it, what it costs, and what we expect it to do in two weeks.

If it does not do that, we stop it. A remedy that is still on the prescription six months later because nobody reviewed it is not natural care, it is a subscription.

Herbs interact with drugs. Tell us everything your animal is already taking, including anything bought off a shelf.

07

Massage and acupuncture

Hands on soft tissue work and needling for pain, stiffness and nerve cases. Run alongside physiotherapy in the same course, never sold as a standalone package.

Most useful in older dogs with hip and spine pain, in post-surgical stiffness, and in animals on long term painkillers we are trying to reduce.

Sessions are twenty to thirty minutes. Some animals settle into it and sleep. Some do not tolerate needling at all, and when that happens we say so and stop rather than sell you a course.

Typical cases

  • Hip and spine pain in dogs over eight
  • Stiffness four to eight weeks after surgery
  • Disc and nerve cases past the acute phase
  • Muscle wastage on one side after a limp
  • Anxious animals who hold tension in the neck and back

The senior visit

  • Bloods twice a year, not once a crisis
  • Kidney, liver and thyroid tracked over time
  • A written pain score you can repeat at home
  • Weight and mobility targets with numbers
  • Diet matched to what the organs can still do
  • Dental review, because a bad mouth ages an animal fast

08

Senior and palliative care

An animal over eight needs a different kind of visit. Not more tests. Different ones, twice a year, tracked against the last set so we can see the line rather than the dot.

And when treatment stops being the kind thing, we will say that too. A pain plan, a comfort plan, and an honest conversation about the last few weeks. At home, if that is where your animal is calm.

Nobody in this part of Jaipur is doing this properly. We think that is the gap that matters most.

Every price is published.

Consult, vaccination, lab panel, X-ray, physiotherapy course, grooming. The full list is on the wall at reception and on this website.
