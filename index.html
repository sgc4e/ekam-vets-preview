Mindful, natural and integrated care for your family.

Our holistic services

Nine kinds of care, one team, one file. Medicine leads and everything else is layered on top of it.

Holistic wellness exams

A thirty minute examination that covers food, sleep, movement and mood before it covers the complaint. You leave with a written plan, not a memory of one.

Learn more

Physiotherapy

Therapeutic laser, manual therapy and a home routine you can actually do. Measured week by week. Almost no clinic in Rajasthan offers it.

Learn more

In-house lab

Blood work, cytology, X-ray and ultrasound in the same building. Your report is what the machine printed. We do not edit a value for anyone.

Learn more

Homeopathy

Prescribed inside the consult by a registered veterinary surgeon, alongside the medicine and never in place of it. Used where a gentle option helps: chronic skin, anxiety, slow recovery, older animals already carrying a heavy drug load.

Learn more

Herbal and natural medicine

Plant based preparations for skin, digestion and stiff joints. We tell you what is in it, what it costs, and what we expect in two weeks. If it does not do that, we stop it.

Learn more

Massage and acupuncture

Hands on work and needling for pain, stiffness and nerve cases, run alongside physiotherapy. Useful in older dogs and in animals we are trying to move off long term painkillers.

Learn more

Senior and palliative care

Animals over eight need a different visit. Twice yearly bloods, a pain plan, a weight and mobility target, and a diet that matches failing kidneys or a tired heart. When the time comes, an honest conversation about the last few weeks, at home if you want it that way.

Learn more

Our mission

ekam means one.

One roof, one file, one team, one animal at the centre of it. We built this clinic because a dog in Jaipur can get a diagnosis, a drug and a discharge, and still cannot get the eight weeks after. That gap is where most animals lose their recovery.

This is a private practice. We charge a fair price and we publish it, and the free treatment we give street animals is paid for by the clinic itself.

  • Low stress handling. We work on the floor if that is where your animal is calm.
  • The estimate before the treatment. No test and no procedure without your yes.
  • Reports we never edit, in either direction, for any reason.

Why ekam vets

Three things you can hold us to on your first visit.

Unhurried attention
